What the contact form collects

Your name, email, and whatever you choose to write in the message, plus which page you sent it from. The form is handled by Web3Forms, which passes it on to my email.

What I do with it

I use it to reply to you and to plan your celebration if you hire me. I don't sell it, share it or add you to a mailing list. If you'd like me to delete our emails, ask and I will.

Visits to the site

The site doesn't use advertising trackers or cookies. If I add visitor counts, they'll be anonymous totals, like how many people opened the prices page.

Stories and photos

Photos, recordings and stories you share for a celebration belong to your family. I don't post them anywhere without asking first.
