In short

I give families a free guide to what funerals cost here, and if they want help, I plan the celebration.

Grave and Seed helps people in Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti and the rest of Washtenaw County through goodbye: posted prices, free guides, a free call to talk through a plan or budget, and hands-on help when they want it, from planning a celebration of life to leading a secular service. Families hire me directly. There are no referral fees in either direction and no financial ties to any funeral home.

Working together

How I work with you

Chaplains and hospice teams

Families often ask you what happens next. You can point them to my free guides and posted local prices, whether or not they ever hire me. I'm glad to style a remembrance service for your team, too.

Clergy and congregations

I can plan the reception after a service so staff and volunteers aren't stretched, or lead a secular service for families who aren't members. When a family wants both, I'm happy to share the service with you. I also offer a free session for congregations on planning the celebration you'd want.

Funeral directors

You handle the body, the permits and the death certificate. I handle the gathering, which can happen later and anywhere. A one-page sheet for funeral directors

Caterers and venues

I'm the family's day-of contact. I arrive early, answer your questions, keep the timeline moving and make sure the room is left the way you want it. One person to call instead of a different cousin each time.

  • What funerals and cremation cost around Ann Arbor, posted prices from local funeral homes with the date each was checked

  • What funerals and burial cost, a printable price sheet
