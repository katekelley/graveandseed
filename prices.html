Your rights

Under the federal Funeral Rule, a funeral home must give you its itemized price list when you visit, and tell you prices over the phone. You can buy a casket or urn somewhere else, and they can't charge you for using it. You can buy only the items you want. More from the FTC

Prices come from each funeral home's own published list on the date shown. Grave and Seed has no financial relationship with any funeral home listed. Spot an error or a newer price? Email {{ c.email }}.
