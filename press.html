About the business

Grave and Seed in a few sentences

Grave and Seed helps people in Ann Arbor and Washtenaw County through goodbye. Founder Kate Kelley publishes the posted prices of local funeral homes and cemeteries side by side, writes free guides for the first days after a death and for planning ahead, and talks families through a plan or budget on a free call, whether or not they hire her. For hands-on help, she plans celebrations of life, serves as a secular celebrant, and leads scattering and interment ceremonies. Grave and Seed is woman- and LGBTQ+-owned and has no financial ties to any funeral home.
