In short

I write and lead a service that sounds like the person you lost.

A celebrant leads a funeral, memorial or celebration of life without being tied to one religion. I meet with you, listen to the stories, write the service, and lead it on the day, so you can be with your people instead of running things.

Many families today don't belong to a congregation, or belong to more than one. Some are spiritual but not religious. Some want a prayer for Grandma and a rock song for her grandson. You don't have to pick one kind of service. We can make room for all of it.

Who it's for

Families a celebrant often helps

Spiritual but not religious

You want something meaningful and a little sacred, without a sermon. We can include silence, nature, poetry, music, or a reading that meant something to them.

Mixed-faith families

One side of the family is Catholic and the other is Jewish, or no one agrees. I help you find a service everyone can stand in, with something for each person.

Not religious at all

A service can be warm and moving with no religion in it. It's about the person: their stories, their jokes, what they cared about.

Not a member anywhere

If your person didn't belong to a congregation, you may not have anyone to call. That's what I'm here for.

Every tradition is welcome

Secular means open to everyone, not against faith

I don't belong to any one tradition's script, so I can make room for yours. Prayers, hymns, scripture, a moment of silence, a candle, a blessing in another language: if it matters to your family, it belongs in the service.

If you have a pastor, priest, rabbi, imam or other faith leader, I'm glad to share the service with them. They can lead the prayers and I can lead the rest. If some of your family holds a faith and some doesn't, we can honor both.

Chosen family is family. Whoever loved this person is welcome to speak.

How it works

From the first call to the day itself

1. A free call

We talk about your person, your family and what you're hoping for. No pressure to hire me.

2. A planning meeting

I listen to stories, ask about readings and music, and learn who wants to speak and who would rather not.

3. A written service

I write the whole service and send it to you. You get a round of edits to make it right.

4. The day

I arrive early, work with the funeral home or venue on timing, and lead the service, so the people closest to them can just be there.

I can lead a service at a funeral home, a church, a park, a home, a graveside, or anywhere else that fits. Scattering and interment ceremonies are offered too.
