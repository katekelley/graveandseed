The celebration, handled.

I plan celebrations of life and lead secular ceremonies in Ann Arbor and Ypsilanti. Everything about the body, the paperwork and disposition stays with you.

Services and pricesgraveandseed.com/services

Families I can help you serve

  • Direct cremation families who want a gathering weeks or months later, at a home, park, hall or restaurant.
  • Families with no clergy, or a mix of faiths, who want a service built around the person. I can lead it as a secular celebrant.
  • Receptions after your service when the family wants more than your space or staff can offer.
  • Ash scatterings and graveside gatherings for remains that have been waiting at home.

What I don't do

  • Transfers, care of the body, death certificates or permits
  • Caskets, urns or other merchandise
  • Prearrangement contracts or pre-need sales

When a family needs any of that, I send them back to a funeral director.

About me

I've worked at a wedding venue, and I bring a wedding planner's care to memorials. I'm a member of Ann Arbor Friends Meeting.
