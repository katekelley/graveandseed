Printing

  • Use letter paper, and print on both sides, flipping on the long edge.
  • On a phone, open the handout, then use your browser's Share or menu button and choose Print. You can save it as a PDF there too, to email or text.
  • The prices on the handouts update whenever the funeral prices and cemetery prices pages do, so a fresh printout is always current.

If you'd like to hand these out at a hospital, hospice, meeting, library or anywhere else, please do. If you'd like a stack printed for your group, get in touch.
