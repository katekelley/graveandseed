A celebration of life doesn't have to happen at a funeral home, and it doesn't have to happen right away. If the body is cremated or buried first, you can gather three to six weeks later, which opens up far more places.

Questions worth asking any venue: Can we bring our own caterer? Are candles allowed? Do you need a certificate of insurance? How far ahead do you book, and can you hold a date for a day or two? Is it accessible?

Prices are what each venue publishes, checked {{ v.updated }}. Always confirm directly. "Welcomes memorials" means the venue mentions memorials or celebrations of life.
