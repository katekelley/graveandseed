Not everyone who dies was easy to love. Maybe they were funny and generous, and they also hurt people. Maybe you spent years keeping your distance. A service full of praise can feel like a lie, and skipping a goodbye altogether can feel wrong too.

The Whole Story is a gathering that tells the truth kindly. I'll help you work out what to say and who should say it, so the room can hold love, anger, relief and grief at the same time, and nobody has to pretend.
