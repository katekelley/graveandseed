Food is how a lot of families grieve. The Long Table brings everyone to sit together and eat, the way you would at a holiday, with the person you lost at the center of the conversation.

It's quieter than a party and warmer than a service. It works beautifully in a home, a barn, a garden or a restaurant's private room.
