If the person you lost ran the table, built the worlds, or knew every line of the show, a quiet funeral home viewing might not feel like them. A Last Roll gathers the people who shared those worlds and lets the stories come out the way they actually happened.

It can be playful and still be a real goodbye. Grief and laughter at the same table is normal.
