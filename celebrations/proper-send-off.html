Some people would hate a hushed room. They'd want a party, a drink in hand, and everybody telling the stories they can't tell at church.

A Proper Send-Off is plain and direct: we gather, we raise a glass, we say what they meant to us. I handle the venue, the vendors and the timeline so the people closest to them can just be there.
