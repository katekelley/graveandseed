The federal Funeral Rule that makes funeral homes hand you a price list doesn't cover most cemeteries (FTC guide). Before you buy a grave, ask for every charge in writing: the grave, opening and closing, Saturday or winter fees, the foundation and marker setting, and whether they'll buy the grave back if you change your mind.
